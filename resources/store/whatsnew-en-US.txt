Reading lists: your own reading plans and collections of passages.

• Split a plan into days — or just keep a list of passages you come back to.
• Read aloud like a playlist: passage after passage, stopping at the end of the list.
• In the reader you page through the list instead of the whole Bible.
• Passages tick off as you read or listen. "Continue" picks up where you left off.
• Or let the assistant build one: "Make me a 30-day plan through the gospels."
