Bible Assistant

Hear, read, keep and share the Bible — voice-driven, hands-free, offline.

## Full description (max 4000) — 3869 characters

Bible Assistant reads the Bible to you. Say what you want to hear — “Read John 3:16” or “Tell me the story of the lost son” — and the passage is read aloud, every word highlighted as it goes.

And when you would rather read for yourself, it is just as much a quiet, well-set reading Bible.

LISTEN

• Say a passage, or type it — and it is read aloud.
• Word-by-word highlighting, so your eye stays with your ear.
• Keep going automatically if you like: passage after passage, chapter after chapter.
• Quiet background music under the reading voice, mixed separately.
• Next, back, pause and pace by tap; pace and stop by voice too.

HANDS-FREE, WITHOUT LOOKING

Hands-free mode fills the screen with five enormous zones: play and pause in the middle, next and back to the right and left, the microphone along the bottom, exit across the top. Long-press a zone and it says what it does. Made for the car, the kitchen, a walk — and for anyone who cannot see the screen well.

READ

• Flowing prose with paragraphs, not one line per verse.
• Superscript verse numbers and a drop cap at the head of each chapter.
• Page through chapters or scroll endlessly — your choice.
• While reading aloud, the page follows the verse being spoken.
• Make the page yours: paper, sepia or night, text size, line spacing, contrast.

ASK INSTEAD OF SEARCHING

You do not have to know the reference. Ask in your own words — “Which passage says that God is love?” — and the assistant tells you where to find it.

READING PLANS

Build reading plans day by day — or have one made for you: “a plan for the New Testament in 90 days”. A day ticks itself off when its chapter has been read aloud to the end, or when you have taken your time over it in the reader.

KEEP THE VERSES

• Cards built from one verse or several, made to flip and memorize.
• Pin a translation to an individual verse — ideal for holding two renderings side by side.
• Your own notes, colors, text size, hashtags to filter by.
• Boards gather cards by theme — as a grid, a stack, or arranged freely on the corkboard.
• Colored ribbons by voice: “save a gold ribbon here” — and pick up there later.

SHELVES

Make shelves for your own writing — thoughts, prayers, short devotions. They are read aloud like a chapter. Share a shelf by code or link with the people you invite: nothing is listed publicly, and you decide who reads. A shelf can share reading plans, boards and even a voice too. Shelves are for Christian, Bible-centered writing.

VOICES

Echo reads straight away, with nothing to set up; your device's own voice works offline too. With your own OpenAI key, 13 voices open up, each speaking the way you describe; an ElevenLabs key adds especially lifelike voices. Give a voice a name and a face.

4 TRANSLATIONS

English: King James Version.
German: Luther 1912, Schlachter 2000, Elberfelder 1905.

The King James Version and Luther 1912 are already included. Schlachter 2000 and Elberfelder 1905 download with a single tap (1.5–1.6 MB each). Schlachter 2000 is read chapter by chapter: its license allows neither endless scrolling nor auto-play.

OFFLINE

The Bible text lives on your device — you need no connection to read, nor for cards, boards and reading plans. The assistant and the natural reading voices need the internet. Download chapters, plan days and shelf pieces to listen offline.

EVERYTHING BY VOICE

Change translation, voice, pace and music, create cards, fill boards, make reading plans, dictate shelf pieces — all in conversation, without walking through menus.

NO ACCOUNT

No email, no password, no sign-up. Your cards, boards, reading plans and voices stay on your device. For another device, turn on sync and you are given 12 words that bring them there. That is all.

For quiet mornings, long drives and learning by heart — and for anyone who would rather hear the Bible than type it.
