# Play Store listing — en-US (translation)

## App name (max 30)

Bible Assistant

## Short description (max 80) — 66 characters

Hear, read and keep the Bible — voice-driven, hands-free, offline.

## Full description (max 4000)

Bible Assistant reads the Bible to you. Say what you want to hear — "Read John 3:16" or "Tell me the story of the lost son" — and the passage is read aloud, every word highlighted as it goes.

And when you would rather read for yourself, it is just as much a quiet, well-set reading Bible.

LISTEN

• Say a passage, or type it — and it is read aloud.
• Word-by-word highlighting, so your eye stays with your ear.
• Keep going automatically: passage after passage, chapter after chapter, until you stop.
• Quiet background music under the reading voice — music and voice mixed separately.
• Next, back, pause, pace: by tap or just by asking.

HANDS-FREE, WITHOUT LOOKING

Hands-free mode fills the screen with five enormous zones: play and pause in the middle, next and back to the right and left, the microphone along the bottom, exit across the top. Nothing to aim at, nothing to hunt for, no small buttons.

Made for the car, the kitchen, a walk, the bedside table — and for anyone who cannot see the screen well.

READ

• Flowing prose with paragraphs, not one line per verse.
• Superscript verse numbers and a drop cap at the head of each chapter.
• Page through chapters or scroll endlessly — your choice.
• Pick a chapter by tapping, or simply say where you want to go.
• While reading aloud, the page follows the verse being spoken.

ASK INSTEAD OF SEARCHING

You do not have to know the reference. Ask in your own words — "Which passage says that God is love?" — and the assistant names it and opens it for you.

KEEP THE VERSES

• Cards built from one verse or several, made to flip and memorize.
• Pin a translation to an individual verse — ideal for holding two renderings side by side.
• Your own notes, your own text size, hashtags to filter by.
• Boards gather cards by theme; on the corkboard you arrange them freely.
• Coloured ribbons (gold, blue, red, green, purple) remember where you want to pick up again.

8 TRANSLATIONS

English: King James Version, New King James Version, English Standard Version.
German: Luther 1912, Schlachter 2000, Schlachter 1951, Hoffnung für Alle, Elberfelder 1905.

The King James Version and Luther 1912 are already included. The rest download with a single tap (1.4–1.7 MB each).

OFFLINE

The Bible text lives on your device — you need no connection to read. To have it read aloud offline, choose your device's own voice, which works without a connection too. The natural reading voices need the internet.

EVERYTHING BY VOICE

Not only the reading: switch language, change translation, change voice and pace, turn the music on or off, create cards, fill boards, drop a ribbon — all in conversation, without walking through menus.

NO ACCOUNT

No email, no password, no sign-up. On first launch you are given 12 words. Those 12 words bring your cards and boards to any other device. That is all.

OPTIONAL: YOUR OWN OPENAI KEY

You can start immediately without setting anything up. Add your own OpenAI key and you unlock every reading voice and the speaking style.

For quiet mornings, long drives and learning by heart — and for anyone who would rather hear the Bible than type it.
